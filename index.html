Help Guide
Compare Tab
1
Add your products
Each card = one product. Tap Scan Barcode to auto-fill the name and size, or just type them in yourself.
2
Enter the price
Type the price shown on the shelf. If the item is on sale, enter the discount amount and choose % (percent off) or $ (dollars off).
3
Tap COMPARE
The best deal turns green. If two items are the same price per unit, both show a TIE badge. The result box below explains the winner.
Go to Settings (toolbar) to change how many product cards show at once — up to 8.
Grocery Tab
1
Create a list
Tap New List on the left sidebar and give it a name — like "Weekly Shop" or "Costco Run".
2
Add items
Use the form to add items with a quantity, price, store, and any notes (e.g. "organic only"). Or ask Penny to add items for you.
3
Shop and check off
While shopping, tap the checkbox next to each item. When done, use Clear Checked to reset the list for next time.
Presets Tab
Presets save your entire Compare setup — all product cards with prices, sizes, and names — so you can reload any comparison in one tap.
1
Save a comparison
After setting up products on the Compare tab, go to Presets and tap Save Current. Give it a name like "Peanut Butter" so you can find it later.
2
Reload anytime
Tap Load on any preset to instantly restore all the product cards. Great for items you compare every week.
Penny (AI Advisor)
1
Ask anything
Penny knows about your household, budget, and preferred stores. Ask things like "What should I buy in bulk?" or "Which store is cheapest for produce?"
2
Share your comparison
Tap Add Compare in the Penny tab to send your current product comparison to Penny and get a recommendation.
3
Manage your grocery list
Just tell Penny what to add — she can create lists, add or remove items, and even suggest quantities based on your household size.
History Tab
Every comparison you run is automatically saved here with the date, winner, and price details. Tap the trash icon to remove individual entries, or Clear All to wipe the full history.
Profile Tab
Update your name, email, and password here. You can also update your household size, shopping frequency, weekly budget, zip code, and preferred stores — Penny uses all of this to give you better advice.
