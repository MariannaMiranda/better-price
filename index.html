Getting Started
1
Scan or Enter a Product
On the Compare tab, tap "Scan Barcode" and point your camera at the barcode. Name and size auto-fill. Or type details manually.
2
Enter the Price
Type the shelf price. Add a discount amount in the Discount field and choose % or flat amount.
3
Set Size and Pack Quantity
Enter size per item and how many are in the pack. For single items, leave Pack Qty at 1.
4
Tap COMPARE
The best value card turns green. If two items are exactly equal, both show a TIE badge.
Features Guide
History Every comparison auto-saves to your account. Each email address has its own separate history.
Grocery Lists Create custom lists (Weekly Grocery, Party Snacks, etc.). Add items with name, price, store, quantity, and notes. Duplicate items across lists. Tap "Uncheck All" to reset for next trip.
Presets Save a Compare setup and reload it any time. Great for items you compare repeatedly.
Export After comparing, tap Export to share or copy results.
Per-Account Data History, grocery lists, and presets are tied to your email. Logging in with a different email shows that account's data only.
Tips
+
Duplicate a Card
The copy icon on each product card duplicates it into the next empty slot — great for same item, different sizes.
+
Duplicate Grocery Items
Each grocery item has a duplicate button — copy it to a different list in one tap.
+
Compare Up to 5 Items
Go to Settings and increase the item count to compare up to 5 products at once.
