Vertex

A simple, friendly vector graphics tool.

Tools

  • P Pen — click to place vertices. Click & drag to make a smooth bezier curve. Double-click, press Enter, or click the first point to finish.
  • V Select — click a shape, then drag to move. Shift+click to multi-select.
  • A Edit Vertices — drag individual points or their bezier handles. Click an edge segment to add a vertex.
  • R Rectangle, E Ellipse, G Polygon, L Line — drag to draw. Hold Shift for a square / circle.
  • H or hold Space Pan; scroll-wheel to zoom.

Shortcuts

  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y / Ctrl+Shift+Z redo
  • Ctrl+D duplicate · Delete remove selection
  • [/] send back / bring forward
  • 1 zoom to fit · +/- zoom · 0 100%
  • Esc deselect / cancel pen path

Tips

  • Want curves? With the pen, drag instead of just clicking — you'll see handles appear.
  • To break a smooth handle into a corner, switch to Edit Vertices and toggle the vertex type in the right panel.
  • Export your work as a crisp SVG or a flat PNG using the buttons in the header.
